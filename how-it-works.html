How SkillSwap Works

One account. Two possibilities.

Learn skills from others. Share your skills with others.

01

Create an account

Start your journey with one profile for everything you learn and teach.

02

Choose Learn or Teach

Choose where to start. Switch modes anytime from the top navigation.

03

Find or create a class

Explore a skill you’re curious about or build a class around what you know.

04

Pay or set your class price

Learners see a clear total before checkout. Instructors decide their own price.

05

Learn or teach

Meet online or in person. Small groups make room for feedback and connection.

06

Leave reviews

Reflect on what you learned and help others find their next class.
